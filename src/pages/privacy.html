Privacy notice

Last updated November 15, 2026.

This website

Launch-news sign-up. If you enter your email address to hear when Avid Viewer accounts launch, we store that address and the time you signed up. We use it only to tell you about Avid Viewer launches. We don't sell it or share it with anyone. To be removed, email bugs@avidviewer.app and we'll delete it.

Setup codes. On Set up a TV, your playlist details (or backup file) are encrypted in your browser with the code before they're sent. We store only that encrypted copy, a one-way fingerprint of the code, and a one-way fingerprint of your IP address (to limit how many codes can be made). We can't read your logins or links, and we never see the code. Everything is deleted 30 minutes after the code is made.

No tracking. This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps standard short-term server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

The Avid Viewer app

The app keeps your playlists, logins, lists and settings on your TV. Backups are encrypted with a PIN you choose before they are saved anywhere.

When the app crashes or video keeps freezing, it can send a report to help us fix the problem. Reports leave out your playlist logins and channel or title names. The full details are in the Privacy section of the user guide.

Avid Viewer accounts

What an account stores: your email address; when the account was made and when you last signed in; your choice about watch history; for each signed-in browser, a label such as "Chrome on Windows" and when it was last used; and, for each passkey you add, its name and public key (a passkey's private part never leaves your device).

Signing in: we email you a one-time link, which works once for 15 minutes, or you use a passkey. There are no passwords. Sign-in links and browser sessions are stored only as one-way fingerprints, so a copy of our database couldn't be used to sign in. To limit abuse, we also keep a one-way fingerprint of the IP address that asked for each sign-in link, for a day.

Deleting it: Account › Delete account removes the account, its passkeys and every signed-in browser at once. Your TVs keep working with their own playlists.

Features that will come with accounts, such as watch progress across TVs and encrypted cloud backups, will be described here before they're switched on.
