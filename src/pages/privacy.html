Privacy notice

Last updated November 15, 2026.

This website

Launch-news sign-up. If you enter your email address to hear when Avid Viewer accounts launch, we store that address and the time you signed up. We use it only to tell you about Avid Viewer launches. We don't sell it or share it with anyone. To be removed, email bugs@avidviewer.app and we'll delete it.

No tracking. This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, keeps standard short-term server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service.

The Avid Viewer app

The app keeps your playlists, logins, lists and settings on your TV. Backups are encrypted with a PIN you choose before they are saved anywhere.

When the app crashes or video keeps freezing, it can send a report to help us fix the problem. Reports leave out your playlist logins and channel or title names. The full details are in the Privacy section of the user guide.

Accounts aren't available yet. Before they launch, this notice will be updated to explain exactly what an account stores — for example your watch progress and encrypted backups — and how to delete it.
