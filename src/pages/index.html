How it works

Your account is where your setup lives. Your TVs follow it.

  1. 1 · ADD

    Add your playlists

    Add the playlists you already have, on the website or on a TV. Logins are locked with your passkey, so you type them once and the website itself can never read them.

  2. 2 · MAKE IT YOURS

    Profiles, lists and order

    Give everyone a profile with its own favorites and lists, choose what each one can see, and put categories and channels in your order with your names. Drag and drop on the website, or use the remote.

  3. 3 · SEND TO YOUR TVS

    Every TV follows

    Choose which TVs get which profiles. Changes reach them within a minute while Avid Viewer is open, and the most recent change wins, wherever it was made.

Cloud backups

  • Backs up on its own every day — playlists, profiles, lists and settings
  • Locked with your PIN before it leaves the TV. We can't open it.
  • New TV? Link it, pick a backup, and you're back in minutes
  • One backup per TV, its most recent, kept for 30 days after you remove a TV
