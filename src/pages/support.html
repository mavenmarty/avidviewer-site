Common questions

Does Avid Viewer come with channels?

No. Avid Viewer is a player. You add a playlist from your own provider (an Xtream Codes login or an M3U link), and the app organizes it into a guide and a library.

Which devices does it run on?

Android TV and Fire TV today. Apps for iPhone and Android phones and tablets, Apple TV and Roku are planned. There won't be a player in the web browser: most providers' streams can't play in one. The website is where you set things up for your TVs.

Will my playlists and lists move to a new TV?

Yes. Link the new TV to your Avid Viewer account (its welcome screen shows a code and a QR code) and it gets your playlists and profiles. To bring over another TV's settings too, restore that TV's backup from your account in Settings › Updates & about › Backup & restore. Each linked TV backs itself up every day.

My iPhone won't make a passkey

Check which browser the page is open in. The Gmail app (and other Google apps) open links in Google's own built-in browser, not Chrome: it has a ✕ at the top left, a ⋯ menu and an Ask bar at the bottom. iPhones and iPads don't let browsers built into apps make passkeys, so it's refused straight away, with no Face ID prompt. Tap ⋯ › Open in Safari (or Open in browser): the sign-in link still works there. To stop it happening, choose a browser in the Gmail app's Settings › Default apps. Safari, Chrome, Firefox and Edge all make passkeys; built-in browsers in other apps (Outlook, Facebook, Instagram) can't either.

Report a problem

The quickest way is from the TV: when the app shows a crash message, choose Report issue. You can also send the last crash from Settings › Updates & about. Reports leave out your playlist logins and channel names.

Otherwise, email bugs@avidviewer.app with your app version (in Settings › Updates & about), your device, and what you were doing when it happened.

For anything else (your account, privacy, or a question that isn't a bug), email support@avidviewer.app.
