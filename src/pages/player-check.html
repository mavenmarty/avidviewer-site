Can your playlists play in a browser?

Before we build a web player, this page checks what this browser can play from each of your playlists: one live channel, one movie and one episode.

  • Private: your passkey unlocks the logins here, in this browser. The browser talks to your provider directly; avidviewer.app never sees the logins, the streams or the results.
  • Short: each test opens one stream, muted, for up to 20 seconds, then closes it. If your provider allows only one connection at a time, a TV watching that playlist may stop briefly.
  • This browser only: results can differ in another browser (Safari plays live channels differently from Chrome, for example).
