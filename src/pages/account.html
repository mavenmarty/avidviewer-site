Welcome to Avid Viewer

One question first

Should your account keep your watch history (where you stopped in each movie and episode) so every TV can pick up where you left off? You can change this, or delete the history, at any time.

Your TVs

    Add a TV

    On the new TV, choose Sign in to your account on the welcome screen (or Settings › Account › Link this TV), then enter the code it shows. Once it's linked, it can restore any of the backups below: copy your setup from another TV without typing a playlist again.

    Backups

    Each linked TV keeps its most recent backup here, locked with the PIN chosen on that TV. TVs back up once a day when something changes. To restore, choose Restore from your account in Avid Viewer › Settings › Updates & about, then use Show PIN here.

      Passkeys

      A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of an email link.

          Delete account
