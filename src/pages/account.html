Welcome to Avid Viewer

One question first

Should your account keep your watch history (where you stopped in each movie and episode) so every device can pick up where you left off? You can change this, or delete the history, at any time.

Your devices

    Add a device

    Open Avid Viewer on the new device: its welcome screen shows a code and a QR code. Choose Add a device and type the code, or scan the QR code with your phone to fill it in (on a device that's already set up: Settings › Account › Link this device). Once it's linked, it can restore any of the backups below: copy your setup from another device without typing a playlist again.

    Backups

    Each linked device keeps its most recent backup here, locked with the PIN chosen on that device. Devices back up once a day when something changes. To restore, choose Restore from your account in Avid Viewer › Settings › Updates & about, then use Show PIN here.

      Passkeys

      A passkey signs you in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of an email link.
