Backups

Each linked device keeps its most recent backup here: playlists, profiles, favorites and settings, locked with the PIN chosen on that device. Devices back up once a day when something changed.

Backups in your account

    Show PIN asks you to sign in again if you signed in more than an hour ago without a passkey.

    Restore a backup on a device

    1. Link the device to your account (Devices › Add a device).
    2. In Avid Viewer: Settings › Updates & about › Restore from your account, and choose any device's backup.
    3. Enter that backup's PIN (Show PIN above).

    Copying another device's backup sets up a new device without typing a playlist. Profiles and playlists from your account then follow what's set on these pages.
